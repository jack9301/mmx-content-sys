title: "Sitting is a diagnosis"
date: "2026-11-11"
tags: ["cpps", "pelvic floor"]
---

Most of my patients with pelvic pain arrive with the same sentence: *it gets worse when I sit.*

We treat it like a footnote. It is not a footnote. It is the most useful diagnostic clue most men will ever give us.

A pelvic floor that has been sitting under load for ten hours a day is a pelvic floor in shortening. It guards. It splints. It tightens the places that are already tight.

If pressure on those muscles reproduces the man's own pain — that is not a coincidence. That is the mechanism, found by hand.

Which is why the treatment that works best is not a drug. It is a pair of hands, and then a chair that does not torture you.
