---
title: "The three-week rule"
date: "2026-11-09"
tags: ["recovery", "habits"]
---

Nothing in pelvic floor work changes in three days. Anyone who tells you it does is selling something.

Three weeks is roughly how long it takes for a chronically guarded muscle to begin to lower its resting tone, and for you to learn the difference between guarding and effort. It is not a cure timeline. It is the point at which you should expect to notice *something*.

If you are inside three weeks and nothing has shifted, that is not a failure. It is information. It usually means the approach needs adjusting, not that you need more willpower.

If you are past three weeks and nothing has shifted at all, then the diagnosis is probably wrong, and you should go looking for a better one.
