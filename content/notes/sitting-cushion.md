A split seat cushion costs less than a month of supplements and works faster than most of them.

This is not a strange claim for a urologist to make. If perineal pressure aggravates your symptoms, then removing perineal pressure is a direct intervention. It is not a wellness hack. It is mechanical logic.

The details matter more than the cushion itself: foam dense enough to actually spread the load, a cut-out so the perineum hangs free rather than bearing weight, and a flat surface so your pelvis stays level. A soft pillow you found in a cupboard does none of that.

Most men resist this for about two weeks, then stop noticing they are doing it. That is the whole goal.
