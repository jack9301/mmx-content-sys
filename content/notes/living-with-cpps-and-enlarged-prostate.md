---
title: "Living with CPPS and an enlarged prostate"
date: "2026-11-14"
tags: ["cpps", "bph", "pelvic floor", "lifestyle"]
---

If you have chronic pelvic pain syndrome (CPPS) accompanied by an enlarged prostate, you will often find that sitting causes significant discomfort. You may also experience poor urinary flow and a sensation of incomplete bladder emptying.

Under these conditions, it is important to avoid alcohol entirely. Be sure to get adequate rest throughout the day, especially taking a short nap in the afternoon. You should also refrain from intense or strenuous exercise and sports, as these activities can aggravate the inflammation.

Therefore, during these periods, you should avoid sitting or standing for extended periods of time. The best position is to lie down as much as possible. If feasible, using a saddle seat, a chair with a cutout in the middle, or a specially designed cushion can help relieve pressure on the prostate.
