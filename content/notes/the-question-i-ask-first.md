---
title: "The question I ask first"
date: "2026-11-02"
tags: ["diagnosis"]
---

Before I ask a man about his symptoms, I ask him what he has stopped doing.

Not what hurts. What he has quit.

Gym sessions. Running. Sitting through his daughter's school play. Sleeping on his side. Long drives.

Symptoms are subjective and people describe them badly — pain scales, vague locations, "down there." Behaviour is harder to fake and much harder to forget.

The man who cannot sit through a two-hour flight has told me more about his pelvic floor than a three-hour description of ache and pressure.

And it tells me what he wants back, which is usually not "pain-free." It is usually "normal."
